Lukasz Wieckowski WFM UPDATE REGARDING BREAKS Hey @All

I would like to inform you about changes regarding the use of your breaks and the statuses related to them. Not everything is changing, so as you will notice, some information will remain the same.

Standard Breaks Each agent is entitled to a total of 30 minutes of break time for 8 hours of work. 3min45sec of break time for each hour of work. This time should be used responsibly and appropriately. 30 minutes means exactly 30 minutes - not 32 or 33. Please be mindful of the time.

Biobreaks - In addition to standard breaks Agents may use Biobreaks, which are short breaks related to physiological needs or brief mental or physical rest. This is not a lunch break, a smoking break, etc. For an 8 hour shift, agents are entitled to 10 minutes of paid Biobreak. Biobreaks should be used responsibly. While Biobreaks do not have a fixed time limit, they should not be overused. A single Biobreak should not exceed more than a few minutes.

The change is intended to ensure fair use and accurate accounting of Biobreaks, as they have been misused by some agents in a manner inconsistent with the intended purpose of this type of break.
