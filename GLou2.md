## Document purpose

This handoff consolidates the information provided by Grace’s father regarding her behavior, emotional regulation, anxiety, social confidence, sleep, learning, extracurricular activities, family interactions, and one isolated episode of hitting her thighs.

The objective is to preserve the full context for continued assessment and the eventual development of practical strategies. This is a working profile, not a diagnosis.

The information currently does not establish:

- ADHD

A pediatrician or qualified child-development professional would need to assess Grace directly before diagnostic conclusions could be made.

- Long school days
- Arriving home as late as 18:30
