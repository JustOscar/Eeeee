# TikTok LIVE Creator Network Support: Conversation Handoff

## Purpose

This handoff summarizes the working preferences, support rules, terminology, market-specific guidance, and notable cases from the conversation. It is intended to help continue the work in a new chat or with another AI provider.

## User profile and role

- The support representative is **Oscar**.
- Oscar works in customer support for **TikTok LIVE Creator Networks / TikTok LIVE Agencies**.
- Oscar frequently supplies an existing macro or a customer message and asks for a personalized response.
- Responses may be required in Spanish, English, Brazilian Portuguese, French, or Urdu.
- Oscar speaks English and may request an English translation of customer messages written in another language.

## Mandatory response preferences

1. When Oscar asks to personalize a support macro, return **only the copy-ready macro**.
2. Do not add introductory commentary such as:
   - “Of course, Oscar.”
   - “Here is the polished version.”
   - “Here is the complete macro.”
3. Do not add explanations, headings, improvement notes, or commentary outside the macro unless Oscar specifically asks for analysis.
4. Do not add a signature such as:
   - “Kind regards”
   - “Oscar”
   - “Customer Support”
5. When appropriate, introduce Oscar at the start. For Spain, the preferred terminology is:

   > Mi nombre es Oscar, del equipo de soporte de Agencias de TikTok LIVE.

6. Preserve HTML formatting such as `<br>`, `<strong>`, and `<a>` when the source macro uses HTML.
7. Keep the tone warm, empathetic, reassuring, concise, and professional.
8. Acknowledge the customer’s concern without exaggerating or assuming emotions.
9. Give clear next steps and explain why information or screenshots are needed.
10. Do not promise outcomes or approval.
11. Do not use emojis for serious or unresolved matters such as application rejection, violations, access restrictions, missing documents, or technical problems.
12. Do not ask for a rating or feedback while:
    - The case remains unresolved.
    - Information is still being requested.
    - Troubleshooting is ongoing.
    - The customer must complete another action.
13. Mention feedback only after the matter has been resolved.

## Preferred closing for open cases

Use this closing when waiting for information:

> I appreciate your time and patience. Once we receive these details, we’ll take a closer look and do our best to help you move forward.

Spanish version used in the conversation:

> Agradezco tu tiempo y paciencia. Una vez que recibamos estos datos, revisaremos el caso detenidamente y haremos todo lo posible para ayudarte a continuar.

Brazilian Portuguese version used in the conversation:

> Agradeço pelo seu tempo e pela sua paciência. Assim que recebermos esses dados, analisaremos o caso com mais atenção e faremos o possível para ajudar você a seguir em frente.

Do not use this investigative closing after a final rejection when no further review or escalation is possible.

## Standard documentation requirements discussed

The usual document checklist supplied in the conversation was:

1. Certificate of incorporation or business registration.
2. Shareholding structure documentation.
3. IDs of key management and personnel, when not shown on the incorporation certificate.
4. ID of the Creator Network or agency owner.
5. ID of the legal representative.
6. A bank account capable of settlement in USD.

Requirements may vary by market. All application details and documents should belong to the same legal entity. Do not advise customers to mix a Spanish sole-trader registration with documents from a US LLC.

## Application timelines

The SOP provided by Oscar generally used a review target of **up to 10 business days**. If an application was only submitted yesterday or very recently, explain that it may be too early for an update, while still offering to collect the required details for an escalation when permitted.

## Application status escalation fields

When a customer has submitted an application and Oscar can request an update, collect:

1. Business market being applied to.
2. Registered region of the business.
3. Full legal name of the business.
4. Creator Network or agency name entered in the application.
5. Business registration number.
6. LIVE Backstage login email.

Do not repeat full personal or registration details unnecessarily in customer-facing replies. Confirm sensitive fields as “received” where possible.

## Application denials: critical rule

When an application has been denied and no specific reason is available:

- Neither Oscar nor the applicant receives the specific denial reason.
- Do not claim that the rejection reason can be found in LIVE Backstage.
- Do not ask for a screenshot to investigate the rejection reason.
- Do not promise escalation or further review when none is available.
- Explain that specific criteria cannot be shared.
- The applicant may submit a new application if desired.
- A new application is independently reviewed and approval is not guaranteed.

Do **not** suggest the following in a denial case:

> Check “Your Information was not approved” in LIVE Backstage for the reason.

That guidance was explicitly corrected by Oscar as inaccurate for these cases.

## Notable workflow lessons

### Website or social profile field

A customer was distressed because the application appeared to request a website. The screenshot guidance indicated that the field could be left empty. Correct response:

- Clarify that the website or business social-media profile field is optional.
- The customer does not need to pay to build a website.
- The customer may leave the field empty and press **Submit**.
- Ask for a screenshot only if a new error appears.

### Webinar access

When a customer could not access a webinar:

- First suggest using the registered email, signing out of other accounts, and trying a private/incognito window.
- If registration remains available, provide the registration link and suggest registering again.
- If registration is confirmed and the online event is scheduled for a future date/time, explain that the join link may not become available until closer to the event.
- Do not claim that the confirmation email contains additional access instructions if Oscar has verified that it does not.

### Joining an existing agency

If a person wants to join an existing TikTok LIVE agency rather than create a new one:

- Select **Team Member/Agent** in LIVE Backstage.
- The existing agency can invite the person to its account.

### LIVE availability by region

For TikTok LIVE or Creator Network features unavailable in a region:

- State that the feature is currently unavailable in the customer’s region.
- Explain that availability varies by location.
- Do not provide workarounds that involve selecting a false country.

### Country or market unavailable in the form

For EU-region applications where the place of incorporation is not listed, collect:

1. Business market being applied to.
2. Creator Network name in the application.
3. Registered region of the business.
4. Business registration number.

Do not make an eligibility decision unless confirmed by the relevant team. Do not recommend a service agent or alternative legal structure without confirmed guidance.

## Key Spain and US entity guidance used in a customer case

The escalation response communicated to one applicant was:

- To apply for an agency in Spain, use a legal entity incorporated and registered in Spain.
- A US LLC can be used for an application to the US market, subject to US-market requirements.
- The selected market, country of incorporation, legal name, registration number, and documents must correspond to the same entity.
- A US LLC should not be used to register an agency for the Spanish market.
- Previous acceptance of self-employed registration does not guarantee acceptance under current requirements because requirements may change.
- Every new application is independently reviewed, and approval is not guaranteed.

## Quality target

The objective is to maximize customer satisfaction through:

- Clear ownership.
- Accurate expectations.
- Concise next steps.
- Genuine empathy.
- No unnecessary requests.
- No false promises.
- No premature feedback request.
- Correct market-specific terminology.

A five-star review cannot be guaranteed. Resolution quality, clarity, and appropriate follow-up matter more than emojis or overly enthusiastic language.
