Lukasz Wieckowski WFM Hi @All Due to global absence policy changes I would like to clearly explain what sick days and absenced actually are, to ensure this is fully understood by everyone.

Sick days are additional days off offered directly by TP. As a result, TP has the right to refuse the use of a sick day on selected days (critical days).

In summary, sick days are a benefit, not a statutory right. Sick days are for organizational purposes only and are used to report temporary unavailability of contractors in order to ensure operational continuity.

No remuneration is paid for a day taken as a sick day.

Each agent is entitled to 6 sick days per calendar year. On such days, the collaborator may inform the Supervisor or the WFM team that they are unavailable to perform the assignment and would like to use one of their available sick days.

For absences reported as a sick day, there is no requirement to provide a medical certificate. Starting from 01.07.2026 there will be 5 types of absences with different definitions:

6 sickdays to use on demand. Planned reported absence at least 7 days before planned shift and marked as a planned absence – Absence UZ in Nice, unplanned reported absence between 7 days to 24 hours before shift marked as an unplanned absence – Unplanned UZ in Nice reported unplanned absence less than 24 hours before shift mark as an unplanned absence – Leave (Unjustified Unpaid) in Nice unreported absence at all marked as Leave (NCNS) in Nice.

Each agent is entitled to 6 sick days per calendar year, which can be used on any day except critical days. Sick days can be used on demand without the need to report them a day in advance.

On critical days, it is not possible to use sick days.

Every absence (after using all 6 sickdays) must be reported no later than the 7 days before your scheduled shift to be planned absence.

All sick days/absences/lateness must be reported by sending an email directly to LiveMRTMDL@majorel.com Any absence or lateness that is not reported to the above email address will be considered unreported.

A medical certificate (L4) does not reduce the number of sick days available.

We understand that unexpected situations happen, and we fully respect that. However, please try to avoid excessive amount of Planned Absences and especially Unplanned/Unreported Absences, as this may affect your future schedules - meaning for example less possibilities to take more additional hours.

If you have any questions, please contact me directly. I will also add answers to the most frequently asked questions in this post as they arise.
