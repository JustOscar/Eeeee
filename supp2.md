# TikTok LIVE Agency Customer Support Handoff

## 1. Agent profile and purpose

- Agent name: **Oscar**.
- Role: Customer support for **TikTok LIVE Creator Networks / Agencias de TikTok LIVE**.
- Main work: personalize macros, answer applicant questions, support onboarding and pre-onboarding, interpret SOPs, prepare escalation summaries, and assist with LIVE Backstage operations.
- Oscar speaks English. Customers may write in Spanish, Portuguese, French, or Urdu. If a customer writes in Urdu, explain the meaning to Oscar in English when requested.

## 2. Mandatory writing preferences

### Copy-ready output

When Oscar asks to personalize a macro or draft a customer reply:

- Return **only the copy-ready customer message**.
- Do not add introductions such as “Of course, Oscar” or “Here is the polished version.”
- Do not add explanations, headings, or notes outside the macro unless Oscar asks for analysis.
- Do not add a signature such as “Kind regards, Oscar, Customer Support.”
- Preserve HTML formatting such as `<br>`, `<strong>`, and `<a>` when useful.

### Tone

- Warm, empathetic, reassuring, concise, and professional.
- Acknowledge frustration without exaggerating or assuming emotions.
- Give clear next steps and explain why requested information is needed.
- Avoid promises of approval, earnings, growth, reach, or resolution.
- Avoid emojis for serious, unresolved, technical, rejection, violation, or document-related cases.
- Do not repeat Oscar’s introduction in every follow-up. In an existing conversation, use wording such as:

> Soy Oscar de nuevo, del equipo de Agencias de TikTok LIVE. Con gusto continuaré ayudándote.

- Avoid time-specific greetings for LATAM because Oscar is in Poland. Prefer `Hola, [nombre]` rather than `Buenos días` or `Buenas tardes` unless local time is known.

### Preferred introduction

For Spain-facing support:

> Mi nombre es Oscar, del equipo de soporte de Agencias de TikTok LIVE.

For ongoing conversations:

> Soy Oscar de nuevo, del equipo de Agencias de TikTok LIVE. Con gusto continuaré ayudándote.

### Preferred open-case closing

English:

> I appreciate your time and patience. Once we receive these details, we’ll take a closer look and do our best to help you move forward.

Spanish:

> Agradezco tu tiempo y paciencia. Una vez que recibamos estos datos, revisaremos el caso detenidamente y haremos todo lo posible para ayudarte a continuar.

Brazilian Portuguese:

> Agradeço pelo seu tempo e pela sua paciência. Assim que recebermos esses dados, analisaremos o caso com mais atenção e faremos o possível para ajudar você a seguir em frente.

Do not use an investigative closing when no further review is possible.

### Feedback and reviews

- Never request feedback while a case is open.
- Never mention reviews while requesting documents, screenshots, troubleshooting, or other customer action.
- Feedback may be mentioned only after the issue has been resolved.
- A five-star review cannot be guaranteed. Focus on resolution, clarity, empathy, and accurate expectations.

### Screenshot requests

- Request full-page screenshots when they genuinely help diagnose an error.
- Do not automatically add warnings about personal or confidential information because this can distract the customer.
- Mention privacy only when asking for IDs, legal documents, registration details, or clearly sensitive material, especially in public Lark groups.

## 5. General application requirements

Applicants normally need:

1. A legally registered business in an eligible country.
2. Certificate of incorporation or registration.
3. Shareholding structure documents.
4. IDs of key management and personnel if not shown on incorporation documents.
5. ID of the Agency owner.
6. ID of the legal representative.
7. A bank account capable of receiving USD settlements.
8. Genuine interest or experience in recruiting and supporting TikTok LIVE creators.

All information must belong to the same legal entity. Do not mix documents from a Spanish sole trader with a US LLC.

Other points:

- Minimum age: 18.
- Application is free. TikTok does not charge to register an Agency.
- A physical office is not required. Use the address on the business licence.
- A legal entity can open only one Creator Network per business market.
- Each person may lead only one Agency per market.
- Agency names accept English letters, numbers, and permitted symbols: `. , ( ) & / ' " _ + -`.

## 6. LIVE Backstage account and application flow

### Create an owner account

1. Go to `https://live-backstage.tiktok.com`.
2. Select **Join Us**.
3. Enter an email address or phone number.
4. Set a password.
5. Click **Send** once.
6. Enter the verification code and select **Register**.
7. Select **Creator Network Owner**.

If joining an existing agency, select **Team Member/Agent** instead.

Important:

- If signing up with email, link a phone number.
- The owner registration phone number should match the official contact information.
- One phone number can be linked to only one Backstage or Creator Network account.
- If already linked, request unbinding from the prior account owner or use a new number.
- Existing agencies can add markets with **Add market**.
- If there is no TikTok BD contact email, answer **No** to the referral/contact question.
- Company website or social profile is optional and may be left blank.

### Legal entity section

- Place of incorporation means the country where the company is legally registered.
- Upload the licence/legal document.
- Upload shareholder and management documents.
- If the same incorporation document covers shareholders or management, use **Use this**.
- Owner and legal representative phone numbers should include the country code.

### Questionnaire and agreement

- There are six open questions.
- If selecting **Yes**, add the required comment or option.
- Accept the contract checkbox before proceeding.
- After onboarding, contract access is under `Administration > Contract > Contract`.

### Operational Capability correction

The **Operational Capability** section is optional for applicants and primarily intended for BD Ops.

- If **Continue** opens Operational Capability, the applicant does not necessarily need to fill it in.
- The applicant may select **Back** to exit.
- Do not automatically tell customers to complete every field in this section.

### Cancel application

- Cancelling withdraws the application.
- Refresh the page if the Cancel Application button does not appear.
- Do not advise cancellation while a recent application is under normal review.

## 7. Review times and escalation

- Formal application review target: up to **10 business days** from the latest submission or correction.
- If submitted recently, explain that it may be too early for an update.
- If still **En revisión**, do not cancel or resubmit.
- After more than 10 business days, collect escalation details.

Required escalation details:

1. Market applied for.
2. Business registration country/region.
3. Full legal business name.
4. Agency name in the application.
5. Business registration number.
6. LIVE Backstage login email.
7. Date of latest submission or correction.

Confirm sensitive items as “received” in customer-facing replies rather than repeating them unnecessarily.

## 8. Application rejection rule

Critical operational rule:

- Oscar and the applicant may not receive the specific denial reason.
- Do not claim the reason is available in LIVE Backstage.
- Do not ask for a screenshot to investigate an unavailable denial reason.
- Do not promise escalation or additional review if none exists.
- Explain that detailed internal criteria cannot be shared.
- The applicant may submit a new application if desired.
- Each application receives an independent review and approval is not guaranteed.

## 9. LATAM rules

### Company eligibility

- LATAM accepts businesses registered in LATAM countries listed under the LATAM market, except Cuba.
- Venezuelan entities are accepted.
- Venezuelan-issued bank accounts are not supported.
- Bank account should be from a traditional bank and receive USD.
- LATAM LLCs do not need a minimum operating history.
- No special commercial activity requirement for an LLC in LATAM.

### Creator eligibility

- Creators must be physically located in Spanish-speaking LATAM countries.
- Cuba and Puerto Rico are excluded from the LATAM creator workflow.
- Prepare at least three creators.
- Creators should be 18+, have no sanctions, and have 0–200,000 followers for regular invitations.
- Recommendation: recruit one creator in the first seven days.
- LATAM recommendation: 30+ creators in the first 30 days.
- Operational expectation: 50–60 creators per month.
- Daily communication with creators is encouraged.

### Mandatory onboarding actions

- Register for the current LATAM webinar.
- After submitting the application, email `paula.bernasconi@tiktok.com` for interview instructions.
- Prepare an operations plan for the interview.
- Activate Lark using the same email as LIVE Backstage.

Do not mix the current HubSpot Lark-group webinar form with the older Eventbrite campaign link unless the specific workflow requires it.

### TikTok Shop

- TikTok LIVE Agency and TikTok Shop are separate programs.
- Internal LATAM guidance states TikTok Shop availability for this workflow is Brazil and Mexico.
- Shop permissions and margin sharing are not linked to the Agency program.

## 10. Spain / ES+ rules

- Agency registration countries: Spain and Andorra.
- Potential creators must be located in Spain or Andorra.
- Creators should be 18+, have no bans, and have 0–200,000 followers.
- Optional preparation while waiting:
  - Configure a Business account.
  - Publish an agency introduction video.

US LLC documentation cannot be used to register a Spain-market agency. A US LLC can apply to the US market, subject to US requirements. Previous acceptance of Spanish self-employment registration does not guarantee current acceptance.

## 11. Other market-specific rules

### Germany / Turkey

- German-market applications require a German-registered company.
- Turkish companies cannot directly apply to Germany.
- Turkish-registered companies apply to the Turkish market.
- Check company type, tax documents, IDs, and duplicate accounts.

### MENA

- A registered company alone is insufficient.
- Registered business activity should fit accepted fields such as marketing, advertising, talent management, events, media production, digital content, live streaming, webcasting, or social-media marketing.
- “General supplies” does not meet the described MENA activity requirement.

## 14. Business Account quick start

- Prepare a clear, current business licence with a matching legal name.
- Switch to Business Account in TikTok settings.
- Business verification usually takes 1–5 business days.
- Do not create multiple accounts to retry.
- Optimize the profile name, bio, image, and introduction.
- A verified account can be linked in `Creators > Scout creators`.
- Common linking errors:
  - Personal rather than Business account.
  - Incorrect/expired username or code.
  - Already linked to another Creator Network.
- One Business Account links to one Creator Network.
- The agency may not use the TikTok logo without written brand approval through TikTok Brand Hub.

## 15. Creator scouting and invitations

Invitation methods:

1. QR code: `Creators > Scout creators > Share application info`.
2. LIVE scouting link.
3. Instant Messages.
4. Manual invitation: `Creators > Manage relationship > Add creators`.

Invitation details:

- Manual invitations require a creator invitation code.
- Code must be correct, for the right account, and used within 24 hours.
- Creator finds invitation under `Creator tools > LIVE Center > Creator Network Center`.
- Check pending invitations under `Creators > Manage relationship > Invited`.
- Application cards remain valid for 14 days.
- After application, the Agency has seven days to review and invite.
- Expired cards can be resent.
- Excessive searches may trigger a temporary search limit; wait before retrying.

Creator eligibility and location:

- Agencies may invite creators only from eligible countries in the Agency’s assigned market.
- Region mismatch may happen because the creator is in a different market, is using a VPN, or is inactive so location cannot be detected.
- Encourage the creator to stop VPN use and remain active/broadcast from the correct market.

## 16. Creator management

- Transfer between managers: `Creators > Manage creators > Transfer creator`.
- Manage creator overview and metrics: `Creators > Manage creators`.
- Relationship status: `Creators > Manage relationship`.
- Export data: `Manage creators > Export data` and check `Notifications > Export records`.
- Edit displayed metrics: `Manage creators > Edit display data`.
- Creator departures are visible under Quit, Removed, Expired, Removed by violation, or Quit by resolution.
- Contract start/end dates cannot be changed after acceptance.
- Renew under `Manage creators > Relationships to expire > Renew`; creator must accept within seven days.
- Region-restricted status appears in Manage Creators. Affected creators may lose certain functions and bonuses.
- Missing Manage Relationships or Manage Creators access must be enabled by the owner.

## 17. Official accounts, scouts, IM, and affiliate accounts

### Official account

- Must be a TikTok Business Account.
- Link from `Creators > Scout creators`.
- Common errors: personal account, wrong username, or linked elsewhere.
- Unlink via the three-dot menu and submit a reason.
- Unlink review: 3–5 working days.
- Maximum three unlink requests per month.

### Scouts and instant messages

- Owner has default scouting permission after linking the official account.
- Authorize scouts in `Creators > Scout creators`.
- IM permissions: `Creators > Scout creators > IM Permissions > Authorize`.
- Daily limit: 1,000 messages per official account.
- Agents generally see only chats they personally opened; owner visibility is broader.

### Affiliate accounts

- Created through `Creators > Scout Creators > Account > Affiliate Accounts`.
- Assign a manager and request one-time credentials.
- Credentials are one-time and expire.
- Mobile app only; no PC/web login.
- Cannot be permanently deleted, but access can be revoked or reassigned.
- Cannot send/receive LIVE gifts.
- LIVE, recharge, withdrawals, and extra scouting functions are not supported.
- Less than 1,000 followers means no profile application link.

## 20. Flares and Promote

### Flares

- Access: `Operations > Flares`.
- Only owners distribute Flares.
- New-version impression quota validity: one month.
- Creator Flare validity after receipt: seven days.
- Expiry reminder: 24 hours before expiry.
- Expired Flares cannot be recovered.
- Active Flare lasts 30 minutes and cannot be paused or withdrawn.
- Only one Flare per LIVE.
- Not supported with LIVE Lineup, OBS, WebApp, or screen share; supported in LIVE Studio.
- Performance is not guaranteed.
- App should be updated and internet stable.
- Application failures and unresolved discrepancies go to L1.5.

### Promote

- Access: `Operations > Promote`.
- Paid traffic tool; coupons may be earned through activities.
- Owner authorizes managers before allocating coupons.
- Creator username is mandatory for creator-specific issues, but not for Agency-only issues.

## 21. Analytics and LIVE reports

Paths:

- Creator Analysis: `Data > Creator Analysis`.
- Team Analysis: `Data > Team Analysis`.
- Creator Network Analysis: `Data > Creator Network Analysis`.
- Weekly LIVE Report: `Data > LIVE report`.

First checks:

- Compare the same date range.
- Account for UTC+0.
- Allow synchronization time.

Definitions:

- Valid LIVE day: more than 60 cumulative LIVE minutes in UTC+0.
- Subscribers-only LIVE counts.
- Multi-guest time as a guest does not count.
- Data is available only from the month the creator joined.
- Backstage does not show per-session Diamond detail.
- Backstage Diamonds may be lower than the app because guest-popularity Diamonds for the host are excluded.
- Viewer Count is unique viewers; Tap Count includes repeated entries.
- Impression means a LIVE appeared on a user’s screen.
- Voluntary quit metrics use a screening window shifted back 14 days.

Unresolved Creator Analysis, Network Analysis, or LIVE Report issues go to L1.5.

## 22. General LIVE Backstage administration

- Workspace is the first page after login.
- Notifications use the bell icon and differ from announcements.
- For notification meaning: **See more > View details**.
- Announcements appear on Workspace; select the title or **View all**.
- Announcements marked **Expired** can be disregarded.
- Page loading: refresh or restart browser, then request screenshots if unresolved.
- Missing notifications or announcements: transfer to L1.5.
- Self-service updates currently include:
  1. Agency name.
  2. Settlement payment information.
  3. Legal company documents / qualification validity.
- For compromised Backstage accounts, collect more information before escalation.
