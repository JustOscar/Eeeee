7. Sole proprietors and self-employed applicants

Do not automatically tell a customer that being registered as an autónomo, sole proprietor, or ditta individuale is sufficient.

Safer wording:

Puedes iniciar la solicitud con la documentación disponible, pero la aceptación de una empresa individual depende de los requisitos actuales del mercado y de la revisión correspondiente.

If a rejection explicitly states that natural persons or sole traders are not accepted:

Explain that the issue is the legal form, not missing documents.
Do not ask the customer to resend more documents for the same sole proprietorship.
Clarify that a formally incorporated company is required.
Explain that the incorporated entity must also be registered in a country eligible for the selected market.

Example from Johan’s case:

An Italian ditta individuale was legally registered.
LIVE Backstage still rejected the entity because it was registered under a natural person.
The notice required a formally incorporated company with shareholding documentation.
Resending Italian tax, VAT, or F24 documents did not change the legal form.

13. Rejection handling

There are two different rejection scenarios.

Scenario A: No specific reason is available
Do not invent a reason.
Do not claim the reason can be found in LIVE Backstage.
Do not ask for screenshots to investigate a reason that is not available.
Do not promise escalation if no additional review exists.
Explain that specific internal criteria cannot be shared.
The applicant may submit a new application if desired.
Approval is not guaranteed.
Scenario B: A specific correction is displayed

When the customer provides an explicit rejection message:

Use the displayed reason directly.
Explain the correction requested.
Do not speculate beyond the displayed text.
Clarify whether resubmission is possible.
Clarify which documents must be retained and which new documents must be added.
