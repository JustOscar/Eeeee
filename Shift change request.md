Lukasz Wieckowski WFM Hi @All I would like to clarify a very important matter.

If you send an email requesting for example to start your shift earlier or to adjust your shift hours and you do not receive a response, you should treat this as a lack of approval. 
Of course, OH are always obliged to respond to your emails; however there may be situations where it is not possible to do so at a given moment.

Nevertheless, there must not be situations where, despite the lack of a response, an agent X decides to come earlier and start their shift early. This constitutes a serious breach of contract.

As contractors, you provide your services during the hours assigned to you, not the ones you set independently. I would like everyone to keep this in mind, as such behavior on the part of an agent will result in immediate consequences.

Another important aspect is that, for example, if you asked by email to start your shift at 14:00 instead of 16:00 and, despite not receiving a reply, you decided to come to work at 14:00, and at 15:17 you broke your leg at the workplace, 
the injury would theoretically occur during working hours and at the workplace. However, in reality you should not have been there at all in that situation, which creates serious issues related to formalities and documentation.
